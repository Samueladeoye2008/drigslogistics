FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a delivery?

You can book a delivery through our website by filling out the booking form or by contacting our customer service team directly.

What are your delivery areas?

We provide delivery services across all states in Nigeria, including both local and interstate deliveries.

How do I track my delivery?

Once your delivery is booked, you will receive a tracking number that you can use to monitor the status of your delivery in real-time on our website.

What types of goods do you transport?

We transport a wide range of goods, including parcels, cargo, bulk freight, and e-commerce products. We also offer specialized handling for sensitive items.
